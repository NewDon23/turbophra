Earn additional $TURBO rewards automatically.

Power and Ultimate plan users can receive additional $TURBO rewards on top of their fixed plan yield — no separate deposit or manual setup required.

Step 01

Deposit in Power or Ultimate

Only 30-day and 60-day plans qualify. Minimum qualifying deposit: 100 USDT.

Step 02

Receive $TURBO Additional Allocation

The protocol allocates an additional percentage of deposit value in $TURBO based on deposit size — on top of fixed plan yield.

Step 03

Split User & Upline

70% of the additional token reward goes to the investor and 30% goes to the referrer / upline.

Step 04

Vest Monthly by Rank

First tranche can be claimable immediately, then remaining tokens unlock monthly by rank.
